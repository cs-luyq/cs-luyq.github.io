About

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology, co-advised by Prof. Shengfeng He and Prof. Guiqing Li. I am currently a research intern with the Orange 3DV Team at Moku Lab, Alibaba. In 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at Singapore Management University, working with Prof. Shengfeng He's group. Before my Ph.D., I received both my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Information Engineering from Guangdong University of Technology, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Qingyun Dai and Prof. Jiangzhong Cao.

My research lies in 3D computer vision and generative models — teaching machines to reconstruct, generate, and interact with 3D worlds from limited visual input. My recent work spans 3D Gaussian Splatting, neural scene representations, and video world models.
